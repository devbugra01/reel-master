# 🎣 Reel Master

A cozy fishing game that runs right in your browser. No download, no install.

Sail out in your little boat, cast your line, catch fish and sell them at the harbor.
Upgrade your gear to reach deeper waters, and stay out after sunset to find glowing fish
that only come out at night.

### ▶️ [Play now](https://devbugra01.github.io/reel-master/)

Works on computers (keyboard) and on phones/tablets (on-screen buttons).

## Features

- **14 fish to collect**, from tiny anchovies to the legendary Sea Monster
- **Big fish fight back:** time your pulls on the spinning needle to reel them in
- **5 upgrades**, each one changes how your gear looks: line, reel, rod, hold and boat
  (from a wooden dinghy to a yacht)
- **Day and night cycle:** the sun slowly sets, the stars come out and night-only fish appear
- **A boat cat** that happily eats the small fish you don't need 🐱
- Cheerful background music and sound effects, all made in code
- Your progress is saved automatically in your browser

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Phone / tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Move the boat | `←` `→` | ◀ ▶ |
| Cast | Hold `Space`, release to throw | Hold 🎣, release |
| Steer the hook | `←` `→` `↑` `↓` | ◀ ▶ ▲ ▼ |
| Reel all the way in | `Space` | 🎣 |
| Fight a big fish | `Space` when the needle is in the green | 🎣 |
| Harbor shop | `E` (at the harbor) | Shop |
| Feed the cat | `C` | 🐱 |
| Sound / music | `M` / `N` | 🔊 / 🎵 |

## Made with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file, with no game engine.
The boats, fish and world are drawn on a `<canvas>`, and all music and sounds are generated with the Web Audio API.
